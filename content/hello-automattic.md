## A bit about me

My name is Ashley Kolodziej (pronounced kuh-LOW-jay). I am an Assistant Creative Director in Boston University's Interactive Design department. It's lovely to meet you!

I've been designing for the web professionally for over a decade. One of my highest priorities is to continue to be able to contribute to a codebase&mdash;yes, as a designer!&mdash;even if it is not my main role. I am passionate about teaching and frontend development, and share my knowledge by speaking at conferences, writing documentation, and teaching design and development classes in the evenings at Boston University.

It used to be I was always learning, and now I'd describe it more as always learning, and also always teaching. 

Most of my experience has been with custom WordPress theme development, and pushing the boundaries of what's possible in WordPress. I love to work where I feel like my work makes a significant difference. One thing I like about my current role is that I have the ability to create and affect change, both through code (by maintaining our in-house theme framework and responding to design and development team requests) and through my position as a leader and manager. While being a manager in a future role is not a requirement for me, I would love to be able to feel like my day to day work makes a real and meaningful difference for others.

As I consider my options for the future, Automattic seems to me like it would be a wonderful fit. I hope you agree and to hear from you soon.

Best,

Ashley Kolodziej
